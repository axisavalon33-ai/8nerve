Recording helper – enter PIN

Record the digits 0–9

Use a quiet room. Hold the tablet about 20 cm from the mouth. Tap Record, say the number once, clearly and calmly, at a normal loudness. Recording stops by itself after 3 seconds (or tap Stop). Silence is cut off and the loudness is evened out automatically. Listen with Play, then tap Save. Use the same speaker for all ten digits of a language.
